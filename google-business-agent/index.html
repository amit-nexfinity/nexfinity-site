How it works

From happy customer to posted review in 30 seconds.

01
Scan

Customers scan the QR code at the counter or tap the short link on a receipt. A page in your colours opens. No app, no sign-up.

02
Tap

They tap a few words about the visit — “Friendly staff”, “Painless treatment”, “Great biryani” — and can pick the stylist, doctor or dishes.

03
Post

The AI drafts a natural review in seconds, different every time. One tap copies it and opens Google to paste and post.

We help real customers describe a real visit. The customer always reads the text and posts it themselves.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

Are these fake reviews?+

No. Real customers describe their own visit by picking the words; the AI only turns those picks into sentences. The customer reads the review and posts it from their own account.

Does my customer need to download an app?+

No. They scan a QR code and it opens in the phone's browser.

Which review sites does it support?+

Google, Zomato, Tripadvisor, Yelp and Facebook.

Will every review sound the same?+

No. The AI rotates its writing style and uses the customer's choices, so every review is different.

Can it reply to my Google reviews?+

Yes. Once your Google Business Profile is connected, new reviews get a reply automatically, with separate wording for good and bad reviews.

How long does setup take?+

We set up your page from your Google listing; you add your logo, staff and services, and we give you print-ready QR codes.
