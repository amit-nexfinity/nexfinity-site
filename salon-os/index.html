Run the front desk

Everything your counter does, in one place.

Billing and GST invoices

Bill in seconds with GST worked out per line, discounts, refunds and a PDF invoice. See who on your team brought in the business.

Online appointments

Customers pick services, a stylist (with photo) and a time. Shop hours, staff days off and double-booking protection built in.

Service catalogue

Set up your price list once — price, duration, GST rate. It feeds your bills, your booking page and your Google profile.

Customer insights

Every customer's spend, visits, last visit and churn risk. See your best customers, and the ones about to drift away.

Customer login by OTP

Know every customer by name, with no passwords and no app to install. Returning customers skip straight in.

WhatsApp receipts

Bills sent on WhatsApp with the invoice and cashback earned, with customer consent and opt-out.

Loyalty & Rewards

Give customers a reason to come back.

Built into every bill, so rewards are earned and spent at the counter — no cards, no separate app.

Cashback wallet

Customers earn points on every paid bill and redeem them on the next, confirmed with an OTP on their phone. Points can expire.

Memberships

Sell paid plans — say, a yearly membership at 15% off. The discount applies on every later bill, and renewals carry on from the end date.

Campaigns

Win back customers who haven't visited in a while, and greet them on birthdays and anniversaries.

Trust and privacy

Your customers' data stays yours.

Made for India: GST invoices, phone-OTP login and WhatsApp, hosted on AWS.

  • Customers sign in with a phone OTP; no passwords are stored.
  • Each business sees only its own customers and data.
  • WhatsApp messages need the customer's consent, and STOP is honoured.
  • Staff names stay in your own reports, never on the customer's invoice.
