FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

What is AI visibility?+

It is how often AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini recommend your business when customers ask for a place like yours.

What do AEO and GEO mean?+

Answer Engine Optimisation and Generative Engine Optimisation: making sure AI assistants find, trust and mention your business in their answers — the AI-era version of SEO.

Can you guarantee I will appear in ChatGPT?+

No one can. We measure honestly, fix what the AI relies on, and show you each month what changed.

How is it measured?+

Through the AI companies' own developer tools. Results can differ a little from what one person sees in the app, which is why each question is asked several times.

What do I need to get the free audit?+

Just your Google listing. The audit runs in minutes and you get a PDF and a shareable link.
